Questions patientsask us about EMFACE®

EMFACE® uses a combination of radiofrequency and high-intensity facial muscle stimulation to target the muscles involved in TMJ/TMD. It helps overactive muscles relax and rebalance, which can ease jaw pain, tension and headaches.

Botox uses injections to temporarily weaken the jaw muscle, and it wears off, so it needs repeating every few months. EMFACE® uses no needles and works to retrain and rebalance the muscles behind your pain.

You recline comfortably while you feel gentle warmth and rhythmic muscle contractions. There is nothing to numb and no needles.
